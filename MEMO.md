# Memo: warranty fraud flag
**To:** Ritu Deshpande  **Re:** What to do about warranty fraud

**Decision.** Don't pay the claims the model ranks highest until the investigation desk has checked them. Use the desk's 40 reviews a month on the highest-scored claims, not at random.

**Why fraud rose.** The 1 May change that auto-approves claims under Rs 2,000 without inspection is the cause. Fraud was about 1% of claims through April, 2% in May, 3% in June. Nearly all of the new fraud is small, uninspected claims.

**Your new-partner hunch is partly right.** In that group, outlets under a year old have about 1 fraudulent claim in 4; older outlets almost none. But the problem is a handful of outlets, not "new partners": seven outlets (SP3160 Pune, SP3232 Jaipur, SP3318 Hubballi, SP3129 Chennai, SP3319 Warangal, SP3286 Mysuru, SP3118 Pune) account for 33 of 36 frauds since May. The other new partners are fine. Meenal is right that we need them in smaller cities.

**On the 97% accuracy target.** I recommend dropping it. About 97% of claims are genuine, so paying everything is already 97% accurate and catches no fraud. A model can hit 97% and be useless.

**The number that matters, in rupees.** Tested on June claims (not used to build it), checking the 40 highest-scored claims found 18 frauds, stopping about Rs 24,700 of payouts at a cost of about Rs 8,400 in goodwill for the 22 genuine claims held. That is about **Rs 620 of fraud stopped per claim checked** (plausible range Rs 400-850; small sample). Checking 40 claims at random would find about 1 fraud. Over July-September we expect roughly 15 frauds per 40 checked.

**Next week.**
1. Ask Meenal's team to call the seven outlets above and review their recent small claims; this may beat any model.
2. Hold the model's top 40 claims each month for investigation; show our service desk the reasons on screen.
3. Consider a quick photo or serial check, rather than a full inspection, for small claims from outlets under a year old.
4. Before the board sees this, let me add the investigation outcomes for July-September so we can retest; the model is built on only about 36 recent frauds and fraud patterns move.
5. Tell Tanmay old Zoho cases with outcome "0" may include undecided ones; ask IT to mark them.
